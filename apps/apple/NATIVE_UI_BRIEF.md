## Validation and Apple acceptance

SwiftPM and the strict Linux model harness cover contracts and coordination, not
SwiftUI rendering. The standalone Foundation presentation/demo checks cover stable
accent assignment and existing synthetic-state transitions. Python source guards
check palette parity, material gates and preserved call sites; they do not prove
working controls. Syntax parsing in DEBUG and CERTIGO_PRODUCTION_DISTRIBUTION
does not type-check SwiftUI/UIKit/MSAL.

Apple acceptance remains required: Xcode 26 SDK compilation, iPhone/iPad captures,
iOS 17 fallback and iOS 26 glass, light/dark, Increase Contrast, largest Dynamic
Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Transparency, Reduce Motion, compact split-view navigation
and keyboard/sheet behavior. No screenshot or visual quality score is inferred
from portable tests.

On a configured test account, verify My Learning opens the confirmed goal; Explore
does not change it; select an answer and switch tabs; resume without losing it;
submit online and inspect the confirmed correction; submit offline and verify
pending has no score; retry without duplicate rewards. Check history refresh/load
more/sign-in recovery, then account switching and deletion recovery using existing
authorized test procedures. Never delete a personal account for visual acceptance.
